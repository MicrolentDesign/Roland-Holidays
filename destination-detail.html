Your clients will encounter crystal-clear turquoise lagoons, white-sand beaches, and overwater bungalows that define luxury travel. As a Roland Holidays wholesale partner, you benefit from our exclusive NNC (Net Net Commission) tier structure – meaning you earn more per booking than through any standard retail channel. Sample Itinerary

5-Day Group Itinerary – Maldives Classic

A fully-escorted 5-day program, customisable for FIT or group bookings. Seaplane transfers included.

Upon arrival at Velana International Airport, your clients are met by our dedicated Roland Holidays representative and transferred to the domestic terminal for their scenic seaplane flight over the atolls. The seaplane journey itself is a highlight – expect gasps and photographs!

  • Seaplane transfer to resort (approx. 20–45 min depending on resort)
  • Welcome drinks and fruit platter on arrival at resort jetty
  • Villa orientation and personalised butler introduction
  • Private welcome dinner on the villa deck (sunset views)

A full day dedicated to the extraordinary marine world of the Maldives. Our preferred excursion operators provide top-quality equipment and certified guides for all water-based activities.

  • Morning house reef snorkeling with marine biologist guide
  • Dolphin watching cruise (pods sighted year-round)
  • Optional PADI intro dive or certified dive excursion
  • Sunset dhoni cruise with canapes and sparkling wine

A purposely slower pace for rejuvenation. The Maldives spa experience is renowned as one of the finest in the world, often set over the water with stunning lagoon views through the treatment room floor.

  • Full-day overwater spa package (90-min couple's massage included)
  • Private beach setup with butler service and chilled beverages
  • Optional sunrise yoga on the overwater deck
  • Fine dining experience – private sandbank dinner under the stars

Experience authentic Maldivian life beyond the resort gates. A visit to a local inhabited island offers genuine cultural immersion that elevates the holiday beyond a beach stay.

  • Guided tour of local inhabited island – school, mosque, fishing harbour
  • Deserted sandbank picnic lunch – private setup with champagne
  • Local artisan market – handcrafted lacquerwork and woven goods
  • Night fishing excursion with traditional Maldivian fishing rods

A relaxed final morning before transfer. Late checkout is included in all Roland Holidays Maldives packages – no 11am scramble for your clients.

  • Farewell breakfast with resort management team
  • Late checkout included (up to 2pm) – relaxed departure morning
  • Seaplane or speedboat transfer to Velana International Airport
  • Agent satisfaction survey + repeat booking incentive distributed

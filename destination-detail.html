Maldives for Travel Agents – Why It Sells

The Maldives remains the world's most coveted luxury beach destination. As a Roland Holidays partner, you gain access to exclusive NNC rates across 100+ contracted resorts, with dedicated support from our Maldives specialist desk.

The Maldives archipelago – with its 26 atolls and over 1,000 coral islands – offers an unmatched product for travel agents seeking high-value, high-margin holiday packages. Your clients will encounter crystal-clear turquoise lagoons, white-sand beaches, and overwater bungalows that define luxury travel. The destination consistently records the highest client satisfaction rates in our portfolio.

As a Roland Holidays wholesale partner, you benefit from our exclusive NNC (Net Net Commission) tier structure – meaning you earn more per booking than through any standard retail channel. Our Elite tier partners earn 42% or above on all Maldives resorts, with no commission clawbacks, transparent pricing, and full allocation flexibility for groups of all sizes.

Whether you're placing a honeymooning couple in an overwater villa, coordinating a group buyout of a private island, or building a bespoke water-sports adventure itinerary, our Maldives desk provides same-day quote turnaround, co-branded marketing materials, and dedicated flight & transfer coordination through our seaplane and speedboat partners.

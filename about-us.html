Our Story & Heritage

Pioneers in Professional Travel Solutions Since 1990

Founded over three decades ago, Roland Holidays has grown from a regional travel specialist into one of the most trusted travel partners in the industry. Our deep-rooted expertise and long-standing supplier relationships allow us to consistently deliver the most competitive rates and premium travel products exclusively to travel agents and tour operators.

We understand the challenges faced by agents in today's market. That is why Roland Holidays is built from the ground up to serve the trade — not the end consumer. Every product, rate, and itinerary we offer is designed to help our agent partners win more business and deliver extraordinary experiences to their clients.
